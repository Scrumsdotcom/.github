## Enterprise AI Platform

SEOP is the architecture of the Scrums.com platform: one control plane for AI agents, models, engineers and infrastructure. It is mission control for engineering and product leaders who orchestrate AI agents, talent, teams, infrastructure and delivery intelligence in one place.

- **[Enterprise Platform](https://www.scrums.com/platform)** — The Enterprise AI Software Engineering Orchestration Platform. It connects the tools, AI agents, models, engineers and infrastructure of an engineering organization, and gives all of them one set of governance controls and one set of delivery metrics.
- **[AI Agent Harness](https://www.scrums.com/platform/agent-harness)** — The engineering layer around a language model that makes it a dependable agent: context, tools, guardrails, runtime and evaluation. Every agent starts with your organisation's context, served through one MCP connector, and authority is enforced in the runtime, not the prompt.
- **[AI Agent Gateway](https://www.scrums.com/platform/ai-gateway)** — One governed endpoint between your applications and the AI they call. It routes each call to the right model provider or deployed agent, enforces policy on the way through, and meters usage and cost per agent, team and model.
- **[MCP Gateway](https://www.scrums.com/platform/mcp-gateway)** — The governed control plane between your tools and your AI agents. One Model Context Protocol endpoint for the organisation: add integrations once, scope them once, and see every call every agent makes.
- **[NextType Lang](https://www.scrums.com/platform/nexttype)** — An AI Research Preview: TypeScript-style contracts with dependent types as the spec. Agents write the code, a reference checker checks the contract, and humans review the spec. There is no compiler yet, and nothing on the page is a measured result.

[See the platform](https://www.scrums.com/platform)

## AI Catalog

Deployable AI for engineering orgs: agents, models, MCP servers, skills, APIs and infrastructure, governed through the Scrums.com Enterprise AI Platform.

- **[Agents](https://www.scrums.com/catalog/agents)** — Browse and deploy AI agents for software engineering and operations, governed through one gateway. The catalog describes them as autonomous and semi-autonomous software workers.
- **[MCPs](https://www.scrums.com/catalog/mcps)** — A registry of MCP servers and connectors that expose tools, context and systems to AI agents, deployable through the MCP Gateway.
- **[Models](https://www.scrums.com/catalog/models)** — Foundation, reasoning, embedding and specialist models, deployable with governance and data residency.
- **[AI Infra](https://www.scrums.com/catalog/infrastructure)** — Runtime, compute, storage, databases, queues and observability primitives for AI workloads, in US and EMEA regions.

[Browse the catalog](https://www.scrums.com/catalog)

## AI Platform Services

When delivery needs people or a managed build, engineering leaders can hire certified engineers or run custom software development through the Software Factory, on the same platform.

### Software Development

[**Software Development**](https://www.scrums.com/ai-development) is the Software Factory: build anything, on one monthly plan. Scrums.com provides end-to-end AI-native software development — custom software, web and mobile apps, platforms and AI features — built by senior engineers with AI agents in every step. Every service runs as scoped workstreams on the same Delivery Plan and backlog.

### Hire AI Engineers

[**Hire AI Engineers**](https://www.scrums.com/hire) covers certified developers, CTOs and teams. AI-certified engineers are forward deployed into your systems and managed through the Scrums.com Enterprise AI Platform for Software Engineering.

- **[Dedicated Teams](https://www.scrums.com/hire/teams)** — An AI development team — lead, forward-deployed engineer, engineers and an agent stack — provisioned as one unit on the platform, shipping from week one. You lead the roadmap; engineers and agents work the same backlog.
- **[Forward Deployed](https://www.scrums.com/hire/forward-deployed-engineers)** — Engineers who bridge the gap between your product and your customer's environment: client-facing, technically deep and domain-aware. Hire software engineers to build your product; hire forward deployed engineers to make that product work inside a customer's world.

## Talent Network

The [Talent Platform](https://talent.scrums.com) is free to join. Skill Hub and Pulse sit alongside roles and your profile for members of the network.

- **[Skill Hub](https://www.scrums.com/careers/skill-hub)** — Free certification paths for AI engineering tools, for members of the Scrums.com talent network. Complete a path, add the provider's certificate to your profile, and clients can search the network by verified skill. - **[Pulse](https://talent.scrums.com/pulse)** — The weekly round-up of what Scrums.com engineers are actually reading: papers, write-ups and releases worth the time, chosen by the people shipping with this tooling every day. It lands every Thursday inside the talent platform (sign-in required).
